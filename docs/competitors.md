# Snow-plow software competitors (public prices, checked Nov 6, 2026)
- **Jobber** (https://www.getjobber.com) – general field service; Core $29/mo annual ($49 monthly), 1 user. Strong quoting, invoicing, payments.
- **Yardbook** (https://www.yardbook.com/pages/pricing) – free Starter, $34.99–$49.99/mo; lawn/landscape-first. The free tier is the main "free" rival.
- **Service Autopilot** (https://www.serviceautopilot.com) – from $49/mo + signup fee; snow dispatch board, master routes, per-push/per-inch billing.
- **Yeti Snow** (https://www.yetisnow.com) – snow-specific; $66.50/mo annual ($95 monthly) for 1–25 sites. Dispatch, proof of service, billing.
- **Plowdesk** (https://www.plowdesk.com) – snow-specific; free (3 storm routes), Contractor $95/mo.
- **Icey Software** (https://iceysoftware.com) – snow-specific; free to 10 sites/5 crew, paid from $119 CAD/mo.
- **LMN** (https://golmn.com) – $297/mo + onboarding; landscape estimating with snow.
- **Housecall Pro** – $59/mo annual; **QuoteIQ** $29.99/mo; **CrewTracker** from $250/mo; **Aspire, Sitefotos, Aurora** quote-only (larger commercial operators).

**Takeaways:** free tiers already exist (Yardbook, Plowdesk, Icey), so "free" alone isn't a differentiator. Weather-triggered dispatch tied to real snowfall data plus auto-quotes from property size are less common in the cheap tools, which is Flake's opening. Many small operators still use texts, spreadsheets or QuickBooks, so the email asks what they use.
